The Exceed Student Index

Thirty-eight questions, about twenty-four minutes, one sitting.

Three things before you start.

  • This does not affect your grade. Not now, not later, not in any form. It exists so you can be told the truth about how your judgment is developing without that truth costing you anything.
  • There are no right answers. Every option is something a reasonable person would do. Pick what you would actually do, not what sounds best.
  • You will take this again in Week 15. The number that matters is not this one — it is what moved.

Your answers save as you go. If you close the tab you can come back to this link and pick up where you left off.

How it is built

Most questions describe a situation and give you four courses of action. Some situations appear twice — once with time and information, once with the clock running and people watching. That is deliberate. The difference between how you answer is the most useful thing this measures.

The last sixteen questions are statements about how you actually operate. Answer for the last month, not for the person you intend to become.

This is a structured developmental diagnostic and it is pre-norming. It is not a psychological or clinical assessment, and it is not used for admission, selection, or any decision about your standing.
